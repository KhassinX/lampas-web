Lampas

The Bible, by lamplight.
A reverent Bible reader that lets you read the text by its own light — original and translation side by side — and find its connections, entirely on your device. Lampas surfaces related passages; it never interprets, paraphrases, or invents a single word. The text is public-domain, presented verbatim.

Coming to the App Store · Free · For iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch

Lampas is built by KhassinX, an independent studio for privacy-first Apple platform apps.

Read the Bible faithfully

Original and translation, side by side

Read the Old and New Testaments with the original-language text and a translation in parallel. Move through any book, chapter, and verse — the words you read are the edition's own, presented verbatim.

Canon, tradition-aware

The canon you read respects your tradition. Lampas does not harmonize divergent versions or flatten differences between traditions — it presents the text as the edition gives it.

Transparent provenance

Every passage carries its source. You always know which public-domain edition you are reading, with attribution per edition. Nothing is paraphrased into Lampas's own voice.

A concordance that never interprets

Ask in your own words

Ask a question in natural language and Lampas finds the passages that relate to it — across the whole Bible — using an on-device semantic concordance. It ranks relevance; it does not author answers.

It surfaces, it never generates

Lampas shows you the verses themselves, word for word. There is no generative model writing about scripture, no paraphrase, no commentary added by the app. It finds connections; the meaning stays in the text.

On-device, always

The text and the search index ship inside the app. Your questions, searches, and reading never leave your device to reach us or any third party. It works offline.

Built privacy-first, given freely

No data collected

Lampas has no servers and no analytics. We collect nothing — no accounts, no sign-in, no tracking. The App Privacy Label reads "Data Not Collected" because nothing is.

Yours on your device

Your reading position, bookmarks, and notes stay on your device. If a future version adds sync, it would use only your own iCloud, under Apple's privacy terms — never our servers, and scripture text would never sync.

Free, with no ads

Lampas is offered pro bono — free, with no advertising and no tracking. An optional tip jar is the only way to support the work, and it is entirely optional.

On every Apple device

Lampas is a native app for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch. Available in English and Spanish at launch. Built with Liquid Glass design across Apple's platforms.
